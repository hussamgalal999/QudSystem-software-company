## Positioning recommendation

Do not market CVPen as simply 'we design a CV'. Sell a measurable, transparent job-application readiness process:
1. Free initial CV scan with a plain-language score and prioritized issues. Never describe the score as an official ATS pass probability.
2. Role-specific rewrite: customer chooses a target role and can provide a real vacancy; keywords and achievements are aligned without fabricating experience.
3. Human quality check: spelling, dates, consistency, ATS-readable structure, Arabic/English quality, editable DOCX and PDF export.
4. Before/after explanation: show what changed and why, with permission and anonymization for any sample.
5. Clear revision policy, delivery promise, privacy policy, and no job/interview guarantee.
6. Upsell only where useful: cover letter, LinkedIn rewrite, second target-role version, interview preparation, and application tracker.

## Suggested offer ladder to test (not final prices)

| Offer | Test price | Role in funnel |
|---|---:|---|
| Free CV health check | EGP 0 | Lead capture and trust; return useful findings without forcing a purchase. |
| CV refresh / ATS structure fix | EGP 199–249 | Entry offer for price-sensitive job seekers. |
| CVPen Pro — role-tailored CV | EGP 499 | Main offer aligned to the user's EGP 500 hypothesis. |
| Career Launch bundle — CV + cover letter + LinkedIn headline/About | EGP 799–999 | Higher average order value; only include work that can be delivered reliably. |
| Additional target-role version | EGP 149–199 | Repeat purchase / add-on. |

Validate willingness to pay with a small test before locking prices. Do not claim a guaranteed ATS score or guaranteed job outcome.

## Revenue math — gross sales, not profit

| Monthly gross target | Orders at EGP 500 | Average orders/day (30 days) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| EGP 30,000 | 60 | 2.0 |
| EGP 100,000 | 200 | 6.7 |
| EGP 200,000 | 400 | 13.3 |

At EGP 500 average order value, 200 orders/month is the arithmetic requirement for EGP 100k gross sales; 400 orders for EGP 200k. This excludes refunds, payment fees, labor, acquisition costs, taxes, and operating expenses.

Illustrative funnel math, not a forecast: if 5% of qualified inquiries buy, 200 orders require 4,000 qualified inquiries/month; at 10%, 2,000. If only 2% of relevant landing-page visitors become qualified inquiries, 2,000 inquiries require about 100,000 relevant visits. Measure the actual funnel before scaling spend.

## Youth-oriented campaign concepts

- 'CV Roast — من غير تجريح': anonymized, permission-based 20-second before/after breakdowns; teach one fix per clip.
- 'ATS Myth vs Fact': fast vertical videos that explain what ATS can and cannot do; avoid fear-based claims.
- 'Job Description Challenge': viewers send a vacancy and receive one useful keyword/skill insight; full CV tailoring remains a paid service.
- 'First Job Kit': student/fresh-graduate bundle with CV, LinkedIn starter, and interview checklist.
- 'One Skill, One Bullet': convert a real project, course, volunteer activity, or part-time job into an honest achievement bullet without inventing experience.

## Channel plan

- Facebook groups and campus communities: useful educational posts, admin permission, no spam or unsolicited bulk messaging.
- TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts: 4-6 short educational clips weekly, reused with platform-native captions.
- Partnerships: university clubs, training centers, student activities, bootcamps, and career offices; offer a free workshop or group scan with explicit consent.
- WhatsApp Business: opt-in inquiry and delivery updates, catalog, FAQs, and referral tracking; no purchased contact lists.
- Search: pages for fresh graduate CV, ATS CV Arabic/English, CV for specific job families, and CV review; every page needs real examples and clear service scope.

## Weekly scorecard

Track: reach, video watch-through, profile visits, landing-page visits, free scans, qualified inquiries, paid orders, average order value, conversion rate, delivery time, revision rate, refund/complaint rate, referral share, gross margin, and acquisition cost.

Do not scale a channel based on views alone. Scale only when paid conversion, delivery quality, and contribution margin are healthy.

## First 14-day experiment

Days 1-2: finalize the offer, sample before/after case with permission, privacy and revision terms, landing page, and WhatsApp intake.
Days 3-7: publish 5 short clips, 3 educational posts, run one campus/community workshop, and offer a capped free scan.
Days 8-10: compare segments (students, fresh graduates, career switchers, experienced professionals) by qualified inquiry and paid conversion.
Days 11-14: double down on the best segment and creative; test one bundle and one referral incentive. Review delivery capacity before increasing volume.
