Sources:

Decision: do not change CVPen prices. No approved current price list was found in connected operational records. Do not use competitor prices as CVPen prices or as a reason to discount. Compete on truthful role-specific editing, human fact-checking, transparent scope, revision boundaries and privacy.

### ATS product truth
Official vendor documentation shows why universal “100% ATS compatible” claims are not defensible:
- Greenhouse lists .doc, .docx, .pdf, .rtf and .txt as accepted candidate upload types, with uploads up to 100 MB. Accepted upload is not a guarantee that every parser extracts every field.
- Lever's official parsing guide lists Word, PDF, RTF, WordPerfect, HTML, MS Office HTML and OpenDocument as parseable formats, and says image files such as JPG/PNG cannot be parsed. It recommends testing whether text can be highlighted in the document.
Sources:
- https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360052218132-Supported-formats-for-resumes-cover-letters-and-other-candidate-uploads
- https://help.lever.co/s/article/Understanding-Resume-Parsing

Product recommendation:
1. Ask the applicant to follow the employer's application instructions first.
2. Deliver a text-based document; test that PDF text is selectable and copyable.
3. Validate reading order and field extraction on the actual target system when test access exists.
4. Report structural checks performed, not a universal ATS score or guaranteed pass.
5. Keep role keywords truthful and grounded in the applicant's evidence.
