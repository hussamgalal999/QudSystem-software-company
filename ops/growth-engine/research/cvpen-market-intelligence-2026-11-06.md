# CVPen Market Intelligence — Evidence-led baseline
**Research date:** 2026-11-06  
**Market:** Egypt first; Arabic and English CV services  
**Purpose:** Build a verified commercial and customer-needs evidence base. This is research, not an approved price list or a forecast.

## 1. Executive findings

1. CAPMAS-reported 2025 unemployment was 6.3%; youth unemployment (15–29) was 13.2%; unemployment among qualified youth was 16.8%. The female rate among qualified youth was 38.3%. These are population-level indicators, not estimates of CVPen's addressable market or demand.
2. The World Bank reported that an average 1.3 million young Egyptians enter the labour market annually while about half a million jobs are created in the same period. This supports a competitive job-search context, but does not prove that CV writing services cause hiring outcomes.
3. ILO's Egypt youth overview identifies skills mismatch, fragmented training/career guidance, informality, mobility and childcare constraints as barriers. CVPen should not position CV editing as a solution to structural job scarcity.
4. Publicly listed Egyptian CV-service prices vary by seller, platform, scope, language, turnaround and revisions. Mohimtak's marketplace snapshot on 6 Nov 2026 reported 11 listings, most prices starting at EGP 75, median EGP 100, and an interquartile range of EGP 75–225. Direct-service examples are higher: CVora EGP 299/499/899; CV Mastery EGP 250/400/600; Matlaa lists CV design at EGP 1,027 and a quick review at EGP 390. These are observed asking prices, not verified transaction prices or equivalent service bundles.
5. ATS claims must be narrow and testable. Lever documents parseable document formats and states it cannot parse image files such as JPG/PNG; Greenhouse documents unsuccessful parsing and formatting-related causes. No vendor documentation supports a universal "100% ATS pass" promise.

## 4. ATS product truth and QA requirements

- Lever's official documentation lists Word, PDF, RTF, WordPerfect, HTML, MS Office HTML and OpenDocument as parseable formats. It says JPG/PNG image files cannot be parsed and suggests checking whether text can be highlighted.
- Greenhouse's official support article documents unsuccessful parsing and identifies oversized files, fake/poorly disguised data and formatting issues as possible causes. It notes that tables, columns, graphics, headers/footers and text boxes can interfere with parsing.
- Accepted upload format is not a guarantee that every field will parse correctly in every employer's configuration.

Sources:
- https://help.lever.co/s/article/Understanding-Resume-Parsing
- https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse
- https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360052218132-Supported-formats-for-resumes-cover-letters-and-other-candidate-uploads

**CVPen QA proposal (not a universal ATS certification):**
1. Follow the target employer's file and application instructions.
2. Confirm the delivered document contains selectable text.
3. Inspect reading order, headings, contact fields, dates and section labels.
4. Check for complex columns, tables, text boxes, graphics and image-only pages.
5. Match job-description language only where the applicant's actual evidence supports it.
6. Test against the named ATS only where authorized test access exists; otherwise label checks as structural checks, not an ATS pass.
7. Human reviewer verifies every factual claim with the customer.

## 5. Customer segments — hypotheses to validate, not asserted customer facts

| Segment hypothesis | Job-to-be-done | Likely anxiety / friction to investigate | Useful CVPen response |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-job graduate | Turn education, projects and training into credible evidence | "I have no experience"; fear of being ignored | Evidence inventory, project descriptions, truthful entry-level CV |
| Experienced applicant | Make experience relevant to a target role | CV is task-heavy or too long | Role-specific editing |
| Career changer | Translate transferable skills | Fear of looking unqualified | Skills mapping with proof and honest gaps |
| Applicant with many rejections | Diagnose presentation and role fit | Uncertainty, discouragement, distrust of guarantees | Structured review, prioritised fixes, no hiring promise |
| Arabic/English applicant | Produce accurate language versions | Translation quality and inconsistent terminology | Human-reviewed localization |
| Remote / Gulf-targeting applicant | Adapt to a target market and role | Unknown conventions, visa/location requirements | Market-specific checklist; no legal or hiring guarantee |
| Returning-to-work applicant | Explain career gap accurately | Fear of judgement | Neutral, truthful chronology and skills evidence |

**Research rule:** Validate each hypothesis with consent-based interviews or voluntarily submitted feedback. Do not infer an individual's emotional state from demographics.

## 6. Customer research plan

### A. Public-source desk research
- Collect job postings from public employer career pages and reputable job boards.
- Extract role title, location, seniority, education, required skills, preferred skills, language, tools, experience, application format instructions and date.
- Do not scrape restricted sites, bypass access controls or collect personal applicant data.
- De-duplicate by employer + role + location + posting date.
- Keep source URL, capture date, exact excerpt and confidence.

### B. Ethical customer discovery
- Conduct opt-in interviews with job seekers across segments.
- Ask about last job application, what was confusing, where they abandoned the process, what they paid for, and what outcome they can verify.
- Avoid leading questions such as "Would you buy our service?"
- Record consent separately; anonymise notes; allow withdrawal.
- Never request national ID, bank credentials or full CV in public comments.

### C. Employer / recruiter discovery
- Interview recruiters and hiring managers only with consent.
- Ask about role-specific evidence, common CV readability issues, application workflow and file instructions.
- Do not claim one recruiter's preference represents all ATS systems or employers.

### D. Competitor audit
Capture package name, price, inclusions, language, turnaround, revision count, refund policy, privacy policy, sample quality, evidence behind ATS claims, and last checked date. Mark inaccessible or unverified items explicitly.

## 7. Customer language and content insight backlog

Themes to test, not claims about all applicants:
- "I have skills but don't know how to prove them."
- "I don't know what to remove or keep."
- "I keep applying with one CV for different roles."
- "I don't understand what ATS actually checks."
- "I need an English CV but worry about inaccurate translation."
- "I don't want someone inventing experience on my behalf."
- "I need to know exactly what I am paying for and when I will receive it."

Content should answer one real question at a time, give a practical example, disclose limits and end with a low-pressure next step.

## 8. Commercial data model and KPI definitions

### Funnel
inquiry → qualified → free_check_started → free_check_completed → scope_confirmed → checkout_started → payment_verified → in_delivery → QA_passed → delivered → accepted → referral_or_closed

### Required KPI definitions
- Qualified inquiry rate = qualified inquiries / total inquiries.
- Free-check completion rate = completed checks / started checks.
- Checkout conversion = verified paid orders / checkout starts.
- Lead-to-sale conversion = verified paid orders / qualified leads (cohort-based).
- AOV = gross order value / paid orders; report discounts and taxes separately.
- Collected cash = settled payments less refunds/chargebacks, not merely checkout success.
- On-time delivery = orders delivered by promised deadline / delivered orders.
- QA defect rate = orders with confirmed QA defects / reviewed orders.
- Revision rate = orders with at least one revision / delivered orders.
- Contribution margin = collected revenue less direct fulfilment costs, payment fees, refunds and variable acquisition cost; do not label this net profit.
- CAC = attributable acquisition spend / new paid customers, only where spend and attribution are complete.

If a source is not connected, report **Unavailable**, never zero.

## 9. Data quality and privacy controls

Every evidence record should include source ID and URL; observed and publication dates; geography and role segment; exact excerpt or numeric value; units, currency and methodology; evidence class (official, primary, marketplace, seller claim, interview); confidence; limitations and recheck date.

Separate:
1. Public market evidence.
2. Voluntarily supplied research/interview notes.
3. Customer order records.
4. CV files and identity documents.

Do not put raw CV text, identity documents, bank/payment credentials or sensitive personal details in the marketing intelligence store. No CV should be sent to an AI provider without informed consent, suitable safeguards, and applicable legal/transfer checks.

## 10. Decisions and next actions

1. Keep CVPen prices unchanged until the owner approves a tested package and price schedule.
2. Build a role-family taxonomy starting with customer support, sales, administration, software/IT, accounting, operations and fresh graduates.
3. Collect 100 deduplicated public job postings as a first market sample; stratify by role family, seniority, language and geography. This is a research sample, not a representative national survey.
4. Complete 10–15 opt-in job-seeker interviews and 5–8 recruiter/employer interviews before treating pain-point hypotheses as validated.
5. Run a 20-provider competitor audit and refresh price snapshots weekly.
6. Create one transparent sample deliverable using synthetic data, clearly labelled as a demonstration.
7. Do not launch paid acquisition or promise hiring outcomes until delivery cost, conversion and contribution margin are measurable.

**Evidence boundary:** This baseline is based on public pages captured on 6 Nov 2026. Seller prices and claims can change; no private CRM, payment or customer interview data was available at research time.
