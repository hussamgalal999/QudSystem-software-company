# QudSystem AI & Workflow Opportunity Assessment — Service Pack
Status: ready for a supervised discovery pilot; not a promise to deploy automation.

## Outcome
A decision-ready report showing where automation may help, what it would cost and risk, and how to test value before investing.

## Deliverables
- One current-state process map.
- Bottleneck and repetitive-work analysis.
- Automation opportunity shortlist ranked by impact, feasibility and risk.
- Proposed system/data-flow architecture.
- Privacy, security, human-approval and failure-mode register.
- Measurement plan with baseline metrics and pilot acceptance criteria.
- Implementation roadmap with dependencies and indicative effort bands.

## Discovery questions
- What starts the process and what ends it?
- Which people, systems and files are involved?
- How many cases per week/month and how long does each take?
- Where do errors, delays and rework occur?
- What data is sensitive or regulated?
- Which actions must always require human approval?
- What is the maximum acceptable monthly operating cost?

## Method
1. Interview the process owner and map the real workflow.
2. Separate measured facts from estimates and assumptions.
3. Identify deterministic automation before introducing an LLM.
4. Assess integration availability, access permissions and data handling.
5. Rank options using value, complexity, failure impact and reversibility.
6. Recommend a small pilot with baseline, success threshold and rollback plan.

## Commercial boundaries
- No guaranteed ROI or percentage savings without measured baseline and pilot data.
- No access to production systems during discovery unless explicitly authorized and safely scoped.
- No handling of customer records, credentials or payment data in the report.
- No production changes, account messages, purchases or external publication.
- Implementation, hosting, model usage and third-party subscriptions are quoted separately.
