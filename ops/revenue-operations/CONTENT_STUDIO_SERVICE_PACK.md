## Delivery workflow
1. Collect business description, audience, geography, offers, differentiators and prohibited claims.
2. Request proof for factual claims, testimonials, certifications, prices and performance numbers.
3. Create a content calendar with channel, objective, format, hook, key message and CTA.
4. Draft captions and scripts in the agreed dialect/language.
5. Run brand, language, factual, privacy and platform-policy reviews.
6. Send a preview to the client for approval; record the approved version.
7. Deliver source copy and visual briefs; publishing is excluded unless a separate authorized publisher and approval process are configured.
8. Track delivery time, revision count, acceptance and margin.

## Guardrails
- No fabricated testimonials, client logos, statistics or before/after claims.
- No fear-based security claims or guaranteed outcomes.
- No scraping private profiles or sending unsolicited bulk DMs.
- No posting, ad spend, account changes or replies without explicit owner/client approval.
- Do not put account passwords, access tokens or customer private messages in the content brief.
