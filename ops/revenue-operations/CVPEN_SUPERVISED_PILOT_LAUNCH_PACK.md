## Intake checklist
- [ ] Explain scope, price, turnaround, revision count and limitations before collecting files.
- [ ] Obtain explicit consent for processing the submitted CV and target job description.
- [ ] Ask the customer to remove national ID, full home address, date of birth, marital status, and unrelated sensitive information.
- [ ] Prefer a redacted text excerpt for the free review.
- [ ] Do not upload customer files to public or anonymous model endpoints.
- [ ] Keep source files in an access-restricted location; do not put CV content in GitHub issues, logs, Telegram, or Qudron long-term memory.
- [ ] Record only the operational lead/order reference in the commercial ledger.
- [ ] Verify payment through provider records before marking an order paid.
- [ ] Human reviewer checks every claim, date, title, number and skill against customer-provided facts.
- [ ] Deliver only after human approval; then follow the retention/deletion policy.

## Service delivery SOP
1. Qualify the request and confirm target role/country/language.
2. Agree on package, exact deliverables, price, turnaround and revision limit.
3. Obtain consent and minimum necessary source material.
4. Create an order record; status remains awaiting_payment.
5. Confirm settlement using provider-authenticated evidence; do not accept SMS/screenshots as proof.
6. Produce draft using only customer-verified facts. Mark unknowns as questions; never invent metrics or credentials.
7. Review grammar, role alignment, ATS-safe structure, privacy and factual accuracy.
8. Generate final DOCX/PDF only after the rendering pipeline has been separately tested; until then deliver an approved text/format agreed with customer.
9. Obtain customer acceptance or one included revision.
10. Delete source material according to the published retention policy and record completion without copying CV contents into analytics.

## Claims we must not make
- No ATS pass percentage or guaranteed ATS acceptance.
- No guaranteed interview, job offer or employment.
- No claim that the Qudron autonomous CV pipeline is live.
- No fabricated testimonials, customer counts, success rates or turnaround statistics.
- No claim of automatic payment or file processing.

## Internal pilot metrics
Track only aggregate operational data: lead source, package, quoted amount, verified settlement amount, time spent, revision count, delivery time, acceptance, complaint/refund and contribution margin. Do not use CV text or identity details as analytics dimensions.
