# QudSystem Service Readiness & Launch Catalogue
Decision date: 4 November 2026

## Executive decision
Launch services as human-led, AI-assisted deliverables, not as a promise that Qudron is deployed or autonomous. The Python runtime and Cloudflare adapter have passed CI, but neither is a verified live production service.

## Recommended first three offers
1. CVPen supervised CV health check: free capped structure review; paid human-reviewed improvement and target-role alignment based only on verified facts. No ATS pass, interview or employment guarantee. Do not collect national ID, full home address or payment credentials. No anonymous/keyless model providers.
2. QudSystem content starter pack: two-week calendar, six captions, two short-video scripts, two visual briefs and approval checklist. Client approves all external publication. No fabricated testimonials or metrics.
3. QudSystem AI/workflow opportunity assessment: one process map, bottleneck analysis, feasibility, privacy/security risks, recommended architecture and measurement plan. No ROI claim without measured baseline.

## Commercial controls
- Written scope, price, turnaround, revision limit, exclusions and acceptance criteria before payment.
- Track lead, proposal, accepted order, payment evidence, delivery, acceptance and complaint separately.
- Count revenue only after verified settlement.
- No personal e-wallet workaround or transaction splitting.
- No autonomous customer messages, publishing, payment actions, refunds, ad spend or production changes.
- Consent-based lead sources only; no purchased contact lists or unsolicited bulk messages.
- Keep CVs out of strategic memory and anonymous model endpoints.
- Every pilot has a named human reviewer and source deletion process.

## Minimal launch checklist
- [ ] Approve one offer and customer segment.
- [ ] Publish truthful scope, price, turnaround, revisions and privacy terms.
- [ ] Prepare anonymized sample with fictional or permissioned data.
- [ ] Create simple lead/order ledger with source and consent.
- [ ] Define payment instructions and manual reconciliation; screenshots are not proof.
- [ ] Run one internal dry-run from request to accepted deliverable.
- [ ] Deliver one supervised pilot order.
- [ ] Record time, direct cost, revisions, acceptance and contribution margin.
- [ ] Automate only after evidence supports it.

## Pricing hypotheses, not final prices
- CVPen refresh: EGP 199–249.
- Role-tailored CV: EGP 499.
- Career bundle: EGP 799–999.
- Content and workflow assessment: quote after scope and delivery hours are defined.
Validate willingness to pay. Prices must cover human QA, model use, payment fees, acquisition, revisions, tax and support.

## Go/no-go
Go when the service is a human-led deliverable with clear scope, safe intake and verifiable output. No-go when it depends on a live integration, production hosting, automated payment verification, customer-data storage or autonomous publishing that is not implemented and tested.

This catalogue is not evidence that customers have been acquired or revenue collected.
