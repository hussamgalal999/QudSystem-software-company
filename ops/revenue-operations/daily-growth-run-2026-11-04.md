## CVPen growth funnel and measurement

No lead or payment connector is currently connected to Qudron. Therefore, actual qualified enquiries, paid orders, AOV, conversion, revenue, CAC and contribution margin are **not available** in this run.

Track the following as the minimum daily funnel:
1. Post impressions/reach and link clicks.
2. Qualified conversations attributable to a post.
3. Checkout starts and provider-confirmed successful payments.
4. Paid orders and AOV.
5. On-time delivery, QA defects, revisions, complaints and refunds.
6. Variable cost per order and contribution margin.

Planning arithmetic only: at an illustrative EGP 799 average order value, EGP 200,000 gross revenue needs about 251 orders/month (~8.4/day across 30 days); EGP 1,000,000 needs about 1,252 orders/month (~41.7/day). These are not forecasts and exclude payment fees, taxes, model/hosting costs, support, human review, refunds and acquisition spend.
